Light meets material.

The hero viewer loads a real glTF Binary asset. Its material channels react to the scene environment and lighting, giving a more convincing result than a flat base-color texture alone.

Drag the model to inspect highlights from different angles. Scroll or pinch to zoom in and see the surface detail.
